Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data YOUR_LEGAL_NAME (“we”, “us”) collects through the Saathi Sutra mobile app and website (the “Service”), why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. It is written to meet India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and the Information Technology Act, 2000.

The short version. A matrimonial profile is meant to be seen. Nearly everything you put in your profile — including your photographs, community, caste, horoscope details, education, and income band — is shown to other members of Saathi Sutra so they can decide whether to express interest. Your phone number, email address, and identity documents are never shown to other members. Do not put anything in your profile that you would not want another member to see.

What we do not collect

  • Your full card number, UPI PIN, CVV, or bank credentials. These are entered on Razorpay’s own checkout page and never reach our servers.
  • Your location. The app does not request location permission; city and state are only what you type.
  • Your contacts, calendar, call logs, SMS, or the list of apps on your device.
  • Audio recordings. The app does not use your microphone.

2. Device permissions

  • Camera — only to take your verification selfie, and only when you start verification.
  • Photos / files — only to let you pick profile photos or identity documents you choose to upload.
  • Fingerprint / Face unlock — if you turn on app lock, your device checks your fingerprint or face. Your biometric data stays on your device; we never receive or store it.
  • Notifications — to tell you about interests, matches, and messages. You can turn these off in your device settings.

3. Sensitive personal data

Religion, caste, community, horoscope details, and identity documents are sensitive. We collect community and horoscope details only because matrimonial matching in India is built on them, and only when you choose to enter them. You can leave any of these fields blank and still use the Service, though your profile will match fewer searches. You can edit or clear them at any time from your profile.

Identity documents and verification selfies are stored in private storage that other members cannot access. They are viewed only by our verification reviewers, used only to verify you, and deleted when you delete your account.

4. Why we process your data (and on what basis)

  • To provide the Service — showing your profile, matching, messaging, subscriptions. Basis: performing our contract with you and the consent you gave at sign-up.
  • To keep members safe — identity verification, photo moderation, abuse reports, blocking, fraud and spam prevention. Basis: your consent (for verification), legitimate interest in a safe platform, and legal obligation.
  • To take payment — processing subscriptions and refunds, keeping financial records. Basis: contract and legal obligation.
  • To communicate — notifications about interests, matches, and messages, plus service announcements. Basis: consent, which you can withdraw in your device settings.
  • To improve the app — crash diagnostics. Basis: legitimate interest.

5. Who sees your data

Other members

Your profile — photographs, community, personal, education, career, and family details — is visible to other registered members. Some details may be blurred or hidden until there is a mutual match or until the viewer holds a paid plan. Your phone number, email address, and identity documents are not shown on your profile.

Service providers

We use the following providers to run the Service. They process data only on our instructions:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, and file storage.
  • Razorpay — payment processing. Governed by Razorpay’s own privacy policy.
  • Expo, Google and Apple push services — delivering push notifications.
  • Sentry — error and crash diagnostics, where enabled. Crash reports are scrubbed of names, emails, phone numbers, and passwords.

These providers may process data outside India. Where they do, we rely on contractual protections and transfer data only to countries not restricted under the DPDP Act.

Others

We disclose data to law enforcement or regulators where legally required. We do not sell your personal data, we do not share it with advertisers, and the app contains no advertising or analytics SDKs.

6. How long we keep it

  • Active accounts — for as long as your account exists.
  • After you delete your account — your profile, photos, messages, and identity documents are removed immediately from search and from other members’ view. Residual copies are purged from active systems within 30 days and from backups within 90 days.
  • Payment records — retained for up to 8 years as required by Indian tax and accounting law, even after account deletion.
  • Safety records — where an account was removed for abuse or fraud, we keep a minimal record to stop the same person re-registering.

7. How to delete your account and data

You can delete your account yourself at any time, directly in the app:

  1. Open the Saathi Sutra app and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Scroll to Your Data and tap Delete my account.
  4. Confirm by tapping Delete forever.

In the same section you can tap Download my data to get a copy of your data before deleting.

If you can no longer sign in, email YOUR_EMAIL from your registered email address with the subject “Delete my account”. We will verify the request and delete your account within 7 days. Full details: Account deletion.

8. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you may:

  • Access a summary of the personal data we hold about you (use “Download my data” in the app).
  • Correct or complete inaccurate data — most of it is directly editable in the app.
  • Erase your data by deleting your account, subject to the retention periods above.
  • Withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawing consent for core profile data means we can no longer provide the Service, so it is equivalent to deleting your account.
  • Nominate another person to exercise your rights in the event of your death or incapacity.
  • Complain to us, and then to the Data Protection Board of India if you are not satisfied.

To exercise any of these, email YOUR_EMAIL from your registered email address, or contact the Grievance Officer below. We respond within 30 days.

9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest. Access to production data is restricted to people who need it. Database-level row security restricts what one member can read about another, and identity documents are kept in private storage. Passwords are stored hashed. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password and tell us at once if you suspect your account has been accessed.

If a breach occurs that is likely to affect you, we will notify you and the Data Protection Board of India as the DPDP Act requires.

10. Children

The Service is only for adults of legal marriageable age in India (21 for men, 18 for women) and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has registered, contact us and we will remove the account.
