Delete your Saathi Sutra account

Saathi Sutra · Sri Karanpur, Rajasthan, India

You can delete your Saathi Sutra account and its data at any time. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Option 1: Delete from the app (fastest)

  1. Open the Saathi Sutra app and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Scroll to Your Data and tap Delete my account.
  4. Confirm by tapping Delete forever.

Want a copy first? Tap Download my data in the same section before deleting.

Option 2: Request by email

If you can no longer sign in or no longer have the app, email info@saathisutra.com from your registered email address with the subject “Delete my account” and include your registered phone number. We will verify that the request comes from the account owner and delete the account within 7 days.

What is deleted

  • Your profile and all profile details (personal, community, horoscope, education, career, family, preferences)
  • Your photographs
  • Identity documents and verification selfies
  • Messages, interests, matches, shortlists, and blocks
  • Your sign-in account and device notification tokens

Your profile disappears from search and from other members’ view immediately. Residual copies are removed from active systems within 30 days and from backups within 90 days.

What we keep

  • Payment records (plan, amount, date, payment reference) for up to 8 years, because Indian tax and accounting law requires it.
  • If an account was removed for fraud or abuse, a minimal safety record to stop the same person re-registering.
